NETFLIX SQL PROJECT – 15 BUSINESS PROBLEMS

CORE ANALYSIS

1. Count the Number of Movies vs TV Shows

Business Problem:
Analyze the distribution of content types available on Netflix and determine whether Movies or TV Shows dominate the platform.

------------------------------------------------------------

2. Find the Most Common Rating for Movies and TV Shows

Business Problem:
Identify the most frequently used content rating for each content type (Movie and TV Show) to understand Netflix's target audience.

------------------------------------------------------------

3. Find the Top 5 Countries with the Highest Number of Titles on Netflix

Business Problem:
Determine which countries contribute the most content to Netflix's catalog.

------------------------------------------------------------

4. Identify the Longest Movie Available on Netflix

Business Problem:
Find the movie with the highest duration and analyze long-form content availability on Netflix.

------------------------------------------------------------

5. Find All TV Shows with More Than 5 Seasons

Business Problem:
Identify highly successful and long-running TV Shows available on Netflix.

------------------------------------------------------------

6. Count the Number of Content Items in Each Genre

Business Problem:
Analyze genre distribution and identify the most popular genres on Netflix.

------------------------------------------------------------

7. Find the Top 5 Years with the Highest Number of Indian Content Releases on Netflix

Business Problem:
Determine which years witnessed the highest production and release of Indian content on Netflix.

------------------------------------------------------------

8. List All Documentary Movies Available on Netflix

Business Problem:
Identify documentary movies and analyze Netflix's educational and informational content offerings.

------------------------------------------------------------

9. Find the Top 10 Actors Who Have Appeared in the Highest Number of Movies Produced in India

Business Problem:
Identify the most frequently appearing actors in Indian Netflix movies.

------------------------------------------------------------

10. Categorize Content Based on Description Keywords

Business Problem:
Classify Netflix content into:
- Bad Content → Description contains keywords such as "kill" or "violence"
- Good Content → All other content

Then count the number of titles in each category.
